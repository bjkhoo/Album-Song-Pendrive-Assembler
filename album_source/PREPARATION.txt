========================================================
  PREPARATION GUIDE / 准备指南 (Option 3 双版本)
  《稻、树林和风》原创专辑
  "The Paddy Fields, the Forests and the Wind"
========================================================

Before running album_copier.py, prepare your files as follows:

1. [MP3 版本 - 10 首]
   Place all 10 MP3 files directly in this folder (album_source/):

   01 稻、树林和风.mp3
   02 Thankfulness.mp3
   03 满月.mp3
   04 我苦我乐我转转念.mp3
   05 含羞草的闪亮.mp3
   06 初心.mp3
   07 幸福答卷.mp3
   08 蝴蝶的呢喃.mp3
   09 拥抱无常.mp3
   10 六时·尔时.mp3

2. [WAV 无损版本 - 10 首]
   Place all 10 WAV files inside the WAV/ subfolder (album_source/WAV/):

   album_source/WAV/
   ├── 01 稻、树林和风.wav
   ├── 02 Thankfulness.wav
   ├── 03 满月.wav
   ├── 04 我苦我乐我转转念.wav
   ├── 05 含羞草的闪亮.wav
   ├── 06 初心.wav
   ├── 07 幸福答卷.wav
   ├── 08 蝴蝶的呢喃.wav
   ├── 09 拥抱无常.wav
   └── 10 六时·尔时.wav

   (Note: If the WAV/ folder does not exist or has no files, the program
    will automatically run in Pure MP3 mode.)

--------------------------------------------------------
  IMPORTANT NOTES / 重要提示
--------------------------------------------------------

1. File names MUST match EXACTLY
   (including spaces, Chinese characters, and punctuation)

2. The 2-digit prefix (01, 02, ..., 10) ensures correct
   playback order on car players and Hi-Fi equipment.

3. On the USB Pen Drive, it will produce:
   - Root: 10 MP3 songs (Plug-and-play for car audio & speakers)
   - WAV/ folder: 10 lossless WAV songs (For PC & audiophiles)

--------------------------------------------------------
  QUICK CHECKLIST / 快速检查
--------------------------------------------------------

  [ ] All 10 MP3 files are directly in album_source/
  [ ] All 10 WAV files are in album_source/WAV/
  [ ] File names match exactly (01 to 10)
  [ ] album_copier.py is in the parent folder

========================================================
  Ready? Run: python album_copier.py
========================================================
